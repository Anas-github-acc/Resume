## 1. The Gap Finder:
“Here’s my resume and the job description I’m targeting. Identify every gap — missing keywords, weak verbs, vague claims, and skills they want that I haven’t mentioned. List them in a table: what’s missing vs. what I currently have.”

## 2. The One-Click Rewrite:
“Now rewrite my entire resume tailored to this job description. Match their language, mirror their priorities, and keep it to one page. Don’t make anything up — only use what’s already in my resume, just position it better.”

## 4. The Recruiter Red Flag Scan:
“Read this resume as a recruiter who spends 6 seconds per resume. Tell me: What’s the first thing you notice? What would make you skip it? What cliché phrases would make you roll your eyes? Be brutally specific.”

## 5. The Cold Email Generator:
“Using my resume and this job description, write a 4-line cold email I can send to the hiring manager on LinkedIn. Tone: confident but not desperate. Include one specific thing about the company that shows I did my research.”
